About Us

We build what we believe in

CHIHIZA International was born from a simple conviction: the way we build should honor the land we build on.

For over a decade, we have been designing and developing ecological communities across Latin America — using locally sourced materials like concrete, bamboo, and stone to create homes that are both beautiful and sustainable.

We are not just a real estate company. We are an ecosystem of architects, engineers, builders, and dreamers who believe that development and ecology can coexist.

Our mission is simple: to be the first call a landowner makes when they want to discover the true potential of their property.

Build Trust, Not Sales
We believe that when you build genuine relationships, everything else follows. Our approach is rooted in honesty, transparency, and long-term thinking.
Honor the Land
Every project begins with deep respect for the environment. We use locally sourced materials, minimize waste, and design with nature — not against it.
Create Legacy
We don't build for today. We build communities that will thrive for generations — places where families grow, memories are made, and nature flourishes.
