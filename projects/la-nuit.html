Where we are today

La Nuit is an operating 5-star luxury eco-lodge in La Vega, Cundinamarca. The property is titled, permitted, and fully built to a premium standard: luxury furnishings and linens, professional-grade equipped kitchen, architectural finishes in guadua bamboo and volcanic stone. It is an exceptional asset — currently operated as a personal, lifestyle-oriented project by its owners rather than a fully commercialised hospitality business.

This explains why the numbers do not reflect the asset’s intrinsic value. The current operation carries deliberate restrictions — couples only, weekends only, no pet-friendly, no children — that limit occupancy to 12.5% (approximately 4 nights per month). It was never designed to maximise revenue. Yet the property consistently earns outstanding reviews on Google, TripAdvisor, and Booking, generating significant goodwill that a professional operator can capitalise on immediately.

There is no defect in the asset. There is an opportunity to unlock it.

Investment Structure

Transparent. Protected. Designed for international investors.

Legal clarity: The Certificado de Tradición y Libertad confirms the title is clean — free of liens, disputes, or encumbrances. Land use permits (POT/EOT) are officially approved for tourism and residential use. Papers are up to date.

Ownership structures — flexible for investor needs:

  • Proindiviso: Direct co-ownership share registered on the property title. Each investor holds a proportional undivided interest (cuota parte) in the estate.
  • Fiducia (trust): The property is held in a Colombian fiducia trust, with the investor as beneficiary. Provides asset protection, simplifies transfer, and offers tax planning advantages.
  • Revenue-share: For investors who prefer not to hold title directly, profit-share arrangements tied to the lodge's operating performance are available.

Escrow protection: All investor funds are held in a Colombian fiducia account. Funds are only released upon verified milestone completion.

Resale: Investors may transfer their cuota parte at any time. CHIHIZA facilitates secondary market transactions and maintains a right of first refusal.
