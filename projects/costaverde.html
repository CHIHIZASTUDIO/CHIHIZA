CostaVerde

Pacific Coastal Living

CostaVerde brings CHIHIZA's ecological vision to the Pacific coast of Panama. Located in Panama Oeste, this project offers coastal living with the stability and infrastructure that Panama is known for.

Designed for those who seek both beauty and practicality, CostaVerde combines modern amenities with ecological responsibility. The Pacific coast provides year-round warmth, stunning sunsets, and a lifestyle that balances adventure with tranquility.

Panama offers unique advantages — favorable tax laws, a dollarized economy, and a welcoming environment for international residents. CostaVerde is your gateway to all of this.
