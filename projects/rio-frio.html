Overview

Forest, river, and silence in Tabio's highlands

Río Frío is a mountain sanctuary in the forests of Tabio, Cundinamarca — a place where the sound of flowing water replaces the noise of city life. At the most accessible price point in the CHIHIZA portfolio, this 0.3-hectare property offers the lowest barrier to entry for ecological living in Colombia, 45 minutes from Bogotá.
