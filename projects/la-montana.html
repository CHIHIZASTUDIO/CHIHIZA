The Beginning

Not every project begins with a spreadsheet.

Some begin with a mountain.

La Montaña is born in the highlands of La Vega, Cundinamarca — where the cloud forest meets the sky and the air carries the scent of wet earth and eucalyptus.

Here, the land does not ask to be tamed.

It asks to be listened to.

The slope.
The mist.
The orientation of the morning sun.
The ancient trees.
The sound of water in the ravine.

Every decision must be a response to the place.

This is not about building a hotel.

It is about creating a different way of inhabiting the mountain.

The Vision

Fifteen eco-lodges. One mountain. A single purpose.

Each lodge is conceived as a refuge — a private universe where the boundary between interior and landscape dissolves. 50 square meters of built space, a private terrace, and a view that belongs only to the guest. A communal pool replaces private plunge pools, reducing cost without compromising experience.

A Club House at the heart of the project. Restaurant. Co-work. A place where the community of guests converges without losing the silence that makes the mountain sacred.

Lookouts that extend the experience beyond the room — a main viewpoint, four secondary lookouts woven into the trails. Two kilometers of paths that become the circulatory system of the project.

The mountain is not the backdrop.

The mountain is the project.

The Opportunity

La Montaña is not a single project.

It is the first project of a platform.

CHIHIZA is building the capability to identify, structure, develop, and operate eco-hospitality assets across Colombia. La Montaña is the proof of concept — a template that can be replicated in other landscapes, other climates, other territories.

The mountain is not the destination.

The mountain is the beginning.
