From Break-Even to Premium Destination — A Clear Upgrade Path

Where we are today

La Nuite is an operating 5-star luxury eco-lodge in La Vega, Cundinamarca. It currently generates revenue at USD 143 per night per person (COP 600,000). At this rate, the property is at break-even — operating costs are covered, but no meaningful profit is generated.

This is not a weakness. It is an opportunity.

The property is titled, permitted, and operational. It already operates at a 5-star luxury standard: premium furnishings and linens, professional-grade equipped kitchen, luxury furniture throughout, and architectural finishes in guadua bamboo and volcanic stone. The hard work of establishing the asset is done. What remains is a clear, costed upgrade path that can take nightly rates from USD 143 to USD 357 within 24 months — transforming a break-even operation into a high-yield hospitality destination.

Tier 1 — Current Operations

At USD 143/night, La Nuite covers its costs. Nothing more.

The current operation is a fully equipped 5-star luxury lodge. Premium furnishings and linens, professional-grade kitchen, luxury furniture throughout, architectural finishes in guadua bamboo and volcanic stone. Revenue at 30% occupancy with a USD 143 nightly rate generates approximately USD 15,689 per year. Operating expenses (staffing, maintenance, utilities, marketing, management) consume approximately the same amount.

This means the property is self-sustaining but not profitable. It is a proof of concept — demonstrating that the location, the concept, and the market demand exist. The 5-star standard is already established. What is missing is the investment to unlock the property's true potential through a SPA expansion and additional lodge capacity.

Investment Structure

Transparent. Protected. Designed for international investors.

Legal clarity: The Certificado de Tradición y Libertad confirms the title is clean — free of liens, disputes, or encumbrances. Land use permits (POT/EOT) are officially approved for tourism and residential use. Papers are up to date.

Ownership structures — flexible for investor needs:

  • Proindiviso: Direct co-ownership share registered on the property title. Each investor holds a proportional undivided interest (cuota parte) in the estate.
  • Fiducia (trust): The property is held in a Colombian fiducia trust, with the investor as beneficiary. Provides asset protection, simplifies transfer, and offers tax planning advantages.
  • Revenue-share: For investors who prefer not to hold title directly, profit-share arrangements tied to the lodge's operating performance are available.

Escrow protection: All investor funds are held in a Colombian fiducia account. Funds are only released upon verified milestone completion.

Resale: Investors may transfer their cuota parte at any time. CHIHIZA facilitates secondary market transactions and maintains a right of first refusal.
